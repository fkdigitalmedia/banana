Bananas Foster Cake transforms the iconic New Orleans dessert into a tender, spiced layer cake. The batter is enriched with mashed caramelized bananas, sour cream, and a warm blend of cinnamon and cardamom. A buttery walnut streusel is crumbled over the top, then a rum sauce is drizzled across the surface before baking. The result is a moist, flavorful cake with a crackly, glazed crust and a deep banana-caramel character.

This recipe is built for bakers who want the drama of Bananas Foster without the last-minute stovetop flambé. The bananas are cooked down with butter and sugar, then mashed into the batter, so their sweetness and texture permeate every slice. The rum sauce is stirred together in the same pan, thickening just enough to cling to the streusel as the cake bakes.

A 9-inch square pan keeps the assembly straightforward, and the parchment sling makes it easy to lift the cooled cake out for clean slicing. Serve it warm or at room temperature, and consider a spoonful of lightly sweetened whipped cream or a scoop of vanilla ice cream to echo the classic dessert.

### 🌟 Why You'll Love This Recipe

Mashing the cooked bananas into the batter distributes their moisture and caramel notes evenly, yielding a consistently tender crumb.

The streusel layer adds a crisp, nutty contrast to the soft cake, while the rum sauce drizzled on top bakes into a glossy, flavorful glaze.

Sour cream introduces acidity that tenderizes the crumb and balances the sweetness of the brown sugar and bananas.

## Key Ingredients & Baker's Notes

#### Bananas

Use large, ripe bananas with yellow peels and a few brown spots. They should be sweet and slightly soft but still hold their shape when sliced.

#### Dark rum

Choose a dark or aged rum for its deeper molasses notes. If you prefer not to use alcohol, you can substitute an equal amount of dark rum extract mixed with water, though the flavor will be less complex.

#### Unsalted butter

For the cake batter, the butter must be at cool room temperature—about 65–68°F—so it creams properly. Melted butter is used for the streusel and the sauce.

#### Sour cream

Full-fat sour cream provides the best richness and moisture. Let it come to room temperature before mixing to avoid chilling the batter.

#### Walnuts

Toast the chopped walnuts for 5–7 minutes in a dry skillet or 350°F oven to deepen their flavor before adding to the streusel.

#### Ground cardamom

Use freshly ground cardamom if possible; its floral, citrusy notes complement the banana and rum. Pre-ground cardamom works but may be less potent.

## Step-by-Step Technique & Method

When cooking the bananas for the batter, stir gently to avoid breaking them apart too much; they will be mashed later. Remove them from the pan before adding the rum and half-and-half to prevent them from turning mushy.

Cream the butter and sugar for a full 3–4 minutes on medium-high speed. The mixture should be visibly lighter in color and increased in volume, which incorporates air for a tender crumb.

Add the flour mixture and sour cream alternately in additions, mixing on low speed just until combined. Overmixing after the flour is added develops gluten and can make the cake tough.

### 💡 Expert Tips

  * Line the baking pan with parchment paper, leaving overhang on two opposite sides. This creates a sling that makes it easy to lift the cake out after cooling.
  * For the cleanest slices, chill the cake in the refrigerator for 30 minutes before cutting. Use a sharp, thin knife and wipe it clean between cuts.
  * If the streusel browns too quickly during baking, tent the pan loosely with aluminum foil after the first 40 minutes.

## Delicious Variations & Mix-Ins

#### Pecan Streusel

Replace the chopped walnuts with an equal amount of chopped pecans for a slightly sweeter, softer crunch.

#### Spiced Rum Sauce

Add 1/4 teaspoon of ground cinnamon and a pinch of ground cloves to the rum sauce after removing it from the heat for a warmer spice profile.

#### Banana Foster Cupcakes

Divide the batter among 16–18 paper-lined muffin cups, top with streusel and a drizzle of sauce, and bake at 350°F for 20–25 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.

## How to Serve & Pair

Serve the cake slightly warm or at room temperature. Slice into 12 squares and accompany with a dollop of lightly sweetened whipped cream or a scoop of vanilla ice cream. The rum sauce and streusel are rich, so a simple cup of strong coffee or a glass of cold milk balances the sweetness.

## 📦 Storage, Freezing & Reheating

Store leftover cake in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3–4 days, or wrap tightly in foil or plastic wrap. For longer storage, refrigerate for up to 1 week; bring to room temperature before serving. To freeze, wrap individual slices or the whole cake tightly in plastic wrap and then aluminum foil, and freeze for up to 3 months. Thaw at room temperature. To restore the fresh-baked crumb, toast individual slices or warm gently in a 300°F oven for 5–10 minutes.
