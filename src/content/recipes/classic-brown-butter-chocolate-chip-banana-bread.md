instructions:
  - "Preheat the oven to 350F. Grease and line a standard (9x5”) loaf pan with parchment paper so some hangs over the sides. Set aside."
  - "Add the 1/2 cup (113g) of butter to a saucepan over medium-high heat. Allow to melt and brown, while stirring continuously (~5-8 minutes). As the butter continues to cook, you’ll notice that it starts to foam up across the surface (after about 2 minutes). Stir frequently to prevent the milk solids from burning. Continue cooking, stirring with a spatula, until the milk solids begin to brown (the brown specks at the bottom of the pan) and the color starts to deepen. Once the brown butter reaches a deep golden amber color and smells nutty, remove from the heat and immediately pour into a large bowl. Set aside to cool slightly (about 15-20 minutes)."
  - "In a separate medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt, and cinnamon (if using). Set aside."
  - "Once the brown butter has slightly cooled, add the mashed bananas, brown sugar, Greek yogurt (or sour cream), eggs, and vanilla. Whisk until well combined."
  - "Add the dry ingredients to the wet. Use a wooden spoon or spatula to mix together until just combined and there are no flour streaks remaining, being careful not to over-mix. The batter will be lumpy!"
  - "Then, gently fold in the chocolate chips."
  - "Pour or scoop the batter into the prepared loaf pan and spread out evenly. Take a butter knife or toothpick, and run it down the center of the batter lengthwise. Then if desired, take 2 thin strips of cold butter and place them in the crease that was formed in the center of the batter with the butter knife. This will help create that ‘perfect’ classic crack and dome top while baking!"
  - "If desired, sprinkle a few more chocolate chips on top of the batter."
  - "Place the pan on the middle rack of your oven. Bake at 350F for 60-65 minutes, until golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the middle comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs. Check the loaf around 50 minutes, if the top is starting to brown too quickly, tent the top with foil for the remainder of baking."
  - "Remove from the oven and let cool in the pan for at least 10-15 minutes before transferring to a wire cooling rack."
  - "Slice and serve warm with butter and flaky sea salt, if desired."
  - "Enjoy!"

There's a moment when ripe bananas meet sizzling butter—the kitchen fills with a nutty, caramel perfume that promises something special. This banana bread recipe leans into that promise, enriching the familiar loaf with brown butter's deep, toasted depth. The crumb is tender and moist, thanks to Greek yogurt, while chocolate chips add pockets of bittersweet contrast. It bakes into a loaf with a crackly, golden top and a soft, almost custardy center—the kind that makes you want another slice before it cools completely.

### 🌟 Why You'll Love This Recipe

Brown butter adds a nutty, caramelized flavor that elevates the banana bread beyond the ordinary.,Greek yogurt (or sour cream) ensures a moist, tender crumb without making the loaf heavy.,The combination of overripe bananas and brown sugar creates a deep, sweet, and complex banana flavor.,Baking at 350°F for about an hour yields a high, crackly dome and a perfectly set center.,A generous amount of chocolate chips provides pockets of melty richness, balancing the banana and butter notes.

## Key Ingredients & Baker's Notes

#### Salted butter

Use good-quality salted butter for browning; the salt enhances the flavor. If using unsalted, add an extra 1/4 teaspoon of salt to the dry ingredients.

#### Ripe bananas

Choose bananas with brown spots on the peel for maximum sweetness and flavor. They should be soft enough to mash easily.

#### Brown sugar

Light or dark brown sugar both work; dark will give a deeper molasses flavor. Pack it firmly when measuring.

#### Greek yogurt or sour cream

Use full-fat for the best texture. Bring to room temperature before mixing to ensure a smooth batter. Sourdough discard can also be used (see variations).

#### Eggs

Room-temperature eggs incorporate more evenly into the batter, creating a better emulsion.

#### Vanilla bean paste or extract

Vanilla bean paste adds specks of vanilla and a more intense flavor, but pure extract works perfectly.

#### All-purpose flour

Measure by spooning flour into the measuring cup and leveling off with a knife for accuracy. Do not scoop directly from the bag.

#### Chocolate chips

Semi-sweet chocolate chips are a classic choice, but you can use dark, milk, or even chopped chocolate bars for a more rustic look.

#### Optional: Cinnamon

Adds warmth and spice; if you're a fan of traditional banana bread, this is a great addition.

#### Optional: Cold butter strips

Thin strips of cold butter placed in the center crease help the top crack evenly. Use unsalted or salted, as you prefer.

#### Flaky sea salt

A final sprinkle on top before serving enhances the sweet and salty contrast. Maldon or fleur de sel are excellent choices.

## Step-by-Step Technique & Method

When browning butter, stir constantly and watch carefully—it can go from golden to burnt quickly. The butter is ready when it smells nutty and the milk solids at the bottom are a deep amber color.

Do not overmix the batter once you add the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined and no flour streaks remain; the batter should be lumpy. Overmixing develops gluten, leading to a tough loaf.

### 💡 Expert Tips

  * To test for doneness, insert a toothpick into the center of the loaf. It should come out clean or with a few moist crumbs. If the top is browning too quickly, tent loosely with foil after about 50 minutes.
  * Allow the loaf to cool in the pan for at least 10-15 minutes before transferring to a wire rack. This helps the structure set and makes slicing easier.
  * For clean slices, use a serrated knife and wait until the loaf is completely cool. If you prefer warm slices, be prepared for some crumbling.

## Delicious Variations & Mix-Ins

#### Sourdough Discard Swap

Replace the Greek yogurt or sour cream with an equal amount (120g) of sourdough discard. This adds a subtle tang and helps with leavening. Make sure your discard is at room temperature.

#### Nutty Crunch

Fold in 1/2 cup (60g) of toasted walnuts or pecans along with the chocolate chips. Toast the nuts first to enhance their flavor.

#### Spiced Variation

Add 1/2 teaspoon of ground nutmeg and 1/4 teaspoon of ground cloves along with the cinnamon for a more complex spice profile.

## How to Serve & Pair

Serve slices warm or at room temperature. For an extra indulgence, spread with softened butter and sprinkle with flaky sea salt. This banana bread pairs beautifully with a cup of coffee, tea, or a glass of cold milk.

## 📦 Storage, Freezing & Reheating

Store the banana bread at room temperature for up to 3–4 days in an airtight container or wrapped tightly in foil or plastic wrap. If you prefer, you can refrigerate it for up to 1 week—just bring slices to room temperature before serving or toast them. To freeze, wrap the loaf or individual slices tightly in plastic wrap and then in foil, and freeze for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator or at room temperature. To restore fresh-baked crumb, toast slices or warm gently in the oven or microwave.
