instructions:
  - "Preheat oven to 350F. Line an 8×8-inch pan with heavy duty aluminum foil, spray with cooking spray; set aside."
  - "To a large sauce pan, add the butter, brown sugar, and heat over medium heat to melt butter; whisk or stir nearly constantly to mix the sugar into the melting butter."
  - "Once butter has melted, allow mixture to come to a boil and boil for about 30 to 45 seconds. Pour sauce into prepared pan."
  - "Add the banana slices (slice them on the thicker side or they’ll ‘disappear’ into the sauce as the cake bakes) in an even flat layer for complete coverage over the pan, with about 1/2-inch space between bananas. They don’t have to be perfectly spaced out; set aside."
  - "To a large bowl, add the the egg, sugars, oil, sour cream, vanilla, and whisk to combine."
  - "Add the bananas and stir to combine."
  - "Add the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and stir until just combined; don’t overmix."
  - "Carefully and evenly pour batter over the banana slices, smooth lightly with a spatula as necessary, and bake for about 42 to 45 minutes, or until cake is set in the center and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs but no batter."
  - "Allow pan to cool on a wire rack for at least 3 hours (I cooled overnight) before inverting and serving. If you line with foil, it’s easy to lift cake out with foil overhang, place a cutting board on top of cake, and invert."

This banana bread cake flips the classic loaf on its head—literally. A buttery brown sugar sauce is poured into the pan, topped with thick banana slices, and then covered with a simple single-bowl batter. As it bakes, the bananas caramelize and the cake absorbs their flavor, creating a moist, tender crumb with a sticky, sweet top. The result is a rustic yet elegant dessert that slices beautifully and tastes even better the next day.

What sets this recipe apart is its clever use of ripe bananas two ways: mashed in the batter for moisture and structure, and sliced on the bottom for a built-in topping. The single-bowl method keeps preparation quick and cleanup minimal, while the 3-hour cooling period ensures the cake sets properly for clean slicing. Whether you're a seasoned baker or new to banana bread, this recipe offers a rewarding twist on a familiar favorite.

### 🌟 Why You'll Love This Recipe

The caramelized banana topping adds a deep, buttery sweetness that complements the cake's tender crumb.

Mashed bananas in the batter provide natural moisture, allowing the cake to stay fresh for days.

The single-bowl method minimizes gluten development, resulting in a soft, tender texture without tough edges.

## Key Ingredients & Baker's Notes

#### unsalted butter

Use fresh unsalted butter for the sauce; it melts evenly and lets you control the salt level.

#### light brown sugar

Pack it firmly into the measuring cup to ensure the correct sweetness and moisture in both the sauce and batter.

#### ripe bananas

Choose bananas with plenty of brown spots for maximum sweetness and flavor. For the topping, slice them 1/2 to 3/4-inch thick so they hold their shape during baking.

#### sour cream

Full-fat or light sour cream both work; Greek yogurt can be substituted in equal measure for a tangy note.

#### all-purpose flour

Stir the flour before measuring, then spoon it into the cup and level off to avoid packing in too much.

## Step-by-Step Technique & Method

Boil the butter and brown sugar for exactly 30 to 45 seconds—any longer and the sauce can become too thick or bitter.

Pour the batter gently over the banana slices and smooth it with a spatula; this prevents the bananas from shifting and ensures even coverage.

### 💡 Expert Tips

  * Let the cake cool completely in the pan for at least 3 hours before inverting; this allows the caramel to set and the crumb to firm up for clean slicing.
  * For easy removal, line the pan with heavy-duty aluminum foil and use the overhang to lift the cake out after cooling.
  * If your bananas are very ripe and release extra liquid, mash them well and drain any excess before adding to the batter.

## Delicious Variations & Mix-Ins

#### Chocolate Chip Banana Bread Cake

Fold 1/2 cup mini chocolate chips into the batter after the dry ingredients are just combined. The chips add pockets of melted chocolate without altering the recipe's structure.

#### Spiced Banana Bread Cake

Add 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon and 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg to the dry ingredients for a warm, aromatic twist. These spices complement the caramelized bananas without overwhelming the cake.

## How to Serve & Pair

Slice the cake into 9 squares and serve at room temperature or slightly warmed. It pairs beautifully with a dollop of whipped cream, a scoop of vanilla ice cream, or a drizzle of caramel sauce. For breakfast or brunch, enjoy a slice alongside coffee or tea.

## 📦 Storage, Freezing & Reheating

Store leftover cake in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3–4 days, or wrap tightly in foil or plastic wrap. For longer storage, refrigerate for up to 1 week; bring to room temperature before serving. To freeze, wrap individual slices or the whole cake tightly in plastic wrap and foil, then freeze for up to 3 months. Reheat by toasting slices or warming gently in the oven to restore the fresh-baked crumb.
