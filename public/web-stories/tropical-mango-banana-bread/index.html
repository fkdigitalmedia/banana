Banana Bread Recipe

Tropical Mango Banana Bread

Sweet mango, toasted coconut and a hint of lime zest in one sunny loaf.

bananabreadrecipe.xyz

Why you will love it

Every slice has golden mango in it

Jammy mango pockets, a toasty coconut top, and a crumb that stays moist for days. Just 20 minutes of hands-on work.

At a glance

One loaf, 80 minutes

Prep 20 minutes. Bake 60 minutes. Makes 1 standard loaf — 10 to 12 slices.

Ingredients — the fruit & the base

3 ripe bananas, 1 cup mango

Plus 1/2 cup melted butter, 3/4 cup sugar, 1/4 cup brown sugar and 2 eggs.

Ingredients — the dry mix

Flour, spice & coconut

1 3/4 cups flour, baking soda and baking powder, cinnamon, ginger, lime zest, sour cream and shredded coconut.

Step 1

Heat the oven

350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan and line it with parchment — the mango sugars caramelise and can stick.

Step 2

Whisk the wet ingredients

Mashed bananas, melted butter, both sugars, eggs, vanilla and lime zest — whisk until smooth.

Step 3

Fold the dry mix in

Whisk flour, soda, powder, salt and spices, stir in the sour cream, then fold in two additions. Stop when no dry streaks remain — do not overmix.

Step 4

Fold in the fruit & bake

Gently fold in the mango, coconut and nuts. Bake 58–65 minutes, until deep golden and a toothpick shows a few moist crumbs.

Step 5

Cool before you slice

15 minutes in the pan, then at least 45 minutes on a rack. Slicing warm compresses the crumb — the mango makes this loaf extra moist.

Baker's tip

Toss the mango in flour first

A tablespoon of the measured flour on the diced mango keeps the pieces suspended instead of sinking. If the coconut top browns fast, tent loosely with foil.

Keeps beautifully

2 days on the counter

Airtight, at room temperature for 2 days, then refrigerate up to 5 more days. Slices freeze for up to 3 months.
